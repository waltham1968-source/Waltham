After 25 years in the polling and market research industry, we are exploring a new and more cost-efficient way of estimating markets, opinions and behaviour.

A new approach to market research

We call it AI-Augmented Market Estimation.

Instead of starting every question with a large and expensive survey, the model starts with what we already know: official statistics, existing research, behavioural data and other reliable sources.

AI is then used to combine the information, identify what is missing and build an initial estimate. A smaller sample of real respondents can subsequently be used to test, calibrate and correct the model.

Official data + existing research + behavioural signals + AI inference + human calibration.

The ambition is simple

Better use of existing knowledge.

Fewer unnecessary interviews. Faster answers. Lower cost.

Not replacing people with AI — but using people where their answers add the most value.
