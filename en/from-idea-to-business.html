The business journey

One step should lead to the next.

The chat is the interface. The product is the case that gradually becomes a testable, well-documented business.

01 · Understand

Idea and objective

Output: a clear problem statement
02 · Explore

Competitor analysis

Output: market map and opportunities

Customer, value proposition, price, costs and the least expensive real-world test.
