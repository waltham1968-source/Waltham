The TRUST model

Six dimensions.
One coherent picture.

This is not a decorative score. Each dimension is assessed through concrete, verifiable signals and leads to a prioritised plan for what is worth improving first.

01

Transparency

Ownership, prices, terms, contact routes and a process customers can understand.

02

Identity

Real people, physical presence, accountability and a credible company history.

03

Delivery capability

Cases, competence, outcomes and concrete examples of problems solved.

04

Continuity

Investment, relationships and evidence that the company will still be there tomorrow.

05

Integrity

Keeping promises, communicating honestly and handling mistakes in a fair, visible way.

06

Documentation

References, reviews, images, coverage, professional sources and measurable facts.

Questions

The short answer.

Is this ordinary SEO?

No. Findability is part of the work, but we begin with the company's actual capabilities, evidence and the customer's decision. The objective is understandable, verifiable relevance – not repeated keywords.

Can you guarantee that an AI will recommend the company?

No. Nobody can control a particular answer from a search engine or language model. We can make the company easier to understand and assess through precise services, credible evidence, clear relationships and useful answers.

Is this a large consulting project?

It does not need to be. A first review can focus on one service, one customer group or one concrete problem and end with a small number of feasible actions.

Who is the Trust Bridge for?

Especially companies whose customers buy something that is difficult to assess in advance: advice, crafts, service, property, technical work, treatment or other trust-based services.
