Waltham is an independent advisory firm focused on business development, technology and bringing clarity to complex situations.

Background

From building businesses
to advising them.

The story behind Waltham began in Norway in 1994 with a sports shop specialising in skis and mountain bikes. This laid the foundation for a practical approach to customers, operations and business development.

Waltham was established as a separate company in 2000. Since then, Waltham has helped develop a range of businesses, including InFact AS, and advised many start-ups on their development.

Our experience spans investment, ownership, business management and practical business development. It offers insight into both the opportunities and the difficult decisions that arise when finances, people and different interests need to work together.

For almost ten years, the work has also included leasing commercial premises and advising smaller businesses on their needs and working environments. Close contact with everyday business life has provided practical insight into starting up, running and growing a business, and the challenges involved.
