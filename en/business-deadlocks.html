Understanding before action

Find a way forward.

Waltham helps owners and managers gain an overview of difficult situations, understand the different interests involved and find a realistic way forward.

The issue may be a disagreement about the business’s direction, unclear expectations in a partnership or a project stalled between people and decisions.

The work begins with a confidential conversation and a clear mandate. The aim is to make the situation understandable and the next step possible.

A focused process for finding clarity

From being stuck
to the next step.

  1. 01
    Understand the situation

    What happened, who is involved and what is at stake? Bring the facts, interests and disagreements into view.

  2. 02
    Identify the options

    Which options are realistic, what do they require and what could they mean for the business and the people involved?

  3. 03
    Make the next step concrete

    A basis for making decisions and an agreed way forward: a conversation, a decision or a plan with clear responsibilities.

A clear role

A clear mandate.
A realistic goal.

Waltham can be a confidential sounding board for an owner or manager, or help several parties work through a shared clarification process. The role and scope are agreed at the outset, so everyone knows whose interests the advisory work serves.

The focus is on the business, the dialogue and the options. Where legal or other specialist assistance is needed, the work is scoped in relation to the relevant advisers’ responsibilities.

A good outcome may be better collaboration, a clear decision or an orderly conclusion. The direction depends on the individual situation.
