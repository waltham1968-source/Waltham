          About the preliminary score and risk

          We award points for measured criteria that are met and convert them to 1–10: 1 + 9 × points earned / points measured. Unknown results are excluded and coverage is shown. The initial check covers at most 70% of the full model.

          Risk describes weaknesses in the measured foundation: High below 4, Medium from 4 to below 7, Low from 7. Even Low does not establish that AI recommends you. Results may change when more pages and actual AI responses are examined.

          Opting out of AI training does not reduce the score. Search access and permission for training are separate choices. The perception gap is not included in the visibility score.
