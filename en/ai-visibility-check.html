              Waltham AI Visibility · v1.3

              18 checkpoints. A clear assessment basis.

              Waltham method v1.3 assesses the foundation for being found and understood. The score does not guarantee AI recommendations.

              23% · Technical accessibility

              Access, crawler rules, indexability, sitemap and internal links.

              32% · Identity, relevance and people

              Business identity, services, audience, expertise and professional track records.

              29% · Content and external signals

              Customer answers, consistency, case studies, references and documented external sources.

              16% · Observed AI visibility

              Actual AI responses, factual accuracy and recognition of documented positioning.

              How the score and coverage are calculated

              Each assessed checkpoint receives 0–4 and a fixed weight. Score = 100 × weighted points / assessed weights. Coverage shows how much of the relevant weight has been examined. Unexamined checkpoints do not count as zero.

              The initial check samples up to four pages and shows partial signals. Professional assessments, profiles, registers and AI responses require a documented review. A high preliminary score with low coverage is not a completed analysis.

              Risk concerns only the assessed foundation: High below 40, Medium from 40 to below 70, Low from 70. Material access or identity issues are shown separately. Search access is assessed separately from AI training.
