In development

A new research model for today’s needs.

Waltham is developing a model combining AI research, existing data and focused human verification. We will publish the method only when we can document what it can support.

Why we are developing it

Our ambition is to make credible citizen insight more accessible to local newsrooms. When relevant questions can be identified and verified faster and at lower cost, more people can be heard — more often and about the issues affecting their daily lives. This can strengthen local journalism, public debate and the evidence on which democratic decisions depend.

In development · Media intelligence

From local signals to documented story ideas.

Working title: Waltham Newsroom Signal. Every morning, an AI agent reviews agreed local news sources and open datasets. It identifies patterns, anomalies and unanswered questions — helping the newsroom decide which themes deserve closer investigation.

The product does not write the news or replace editorial judgement. It makes a large source base manageable and shows what should be investigated before a journalist decides whether there is a story.
