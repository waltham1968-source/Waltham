Time to market is a strategic advantage

It can take minutes to become visible in the market. It still takes judgement to build something customers will pay for.

New technology makes it possible to launch a first landing page, offer, lead channel or market test almost immediately — and adapt continuously to real signals. This reverses the development model: less prolonged planning, faster contact with reality.

A fast launch is not the same as a proven market. The objective is to learn earlier, before time and capital are committed to the wrong solution.
