The experience behind the work

25 years of technology and collaboration

Since 2000, I have worked on software development and advanced systems alongside developers and specialists from around the world. I have been fortunate to work with some of the most talented people I have met in the industry, and many of those collaborations continue today.

My core strengths are business understanding, communication and the ability to engage people. I work to make ideas clear, build interest and bring people together around something they want to contribute to.

Throughout my career, I have worked closely with inventors, entrepreneurs and startup communities across the Western world. Over the past five to six years, my work around Lindholm Business Center has also brought me into close contact with many businesses and their everyday needs, challenges and opportunities.

No one can do everything alone. My role is to connect the business and its communication with the technical expertise the task requires.

Waltham is built on that experience and those relationships. They allow us to assemble the right team for each task, from the first idea to a solution that works in practice.

— Nikolai Waltham

Nikolai Waltham’s work with InFact connects more than two decades of market research with technology and human behaviour. Experience in businesses and day-to-day operations provides a practical foundation for turning insight into action.
