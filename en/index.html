The real problem

Most people do not lack information. They lack perspective.

When documents, ideas, people and interests grow in every direction, it becomes difficult to see what matters, what is true and what the next step should be.

An idea without directionGood energy, but an unclear market, customer or first test.
A business without momentumMany possibilities, but too little focus and too few new customers.
A case without shared realityMany documents, opinions and versions of the same story.
A leader without challengeImportant decisions need an honest and experienced thinking partner.

Why it began

Before I built InFact, I was trying to build freedom.

Around the year 2000, I dreamed of sailing around the world with my two young sons. I had bought a 45-foot steel boat and, in many ways, we were ready. What I lacked was an income that could travel with us.

That is why I began developing an automated telephone system that could call thousands of people, ask questions and collect answers. The idea was simple: if I could find an internet café, I could run the work from almost anywhere.

The idea also came from another experience. I had already tried launching several business ideas that I thought were good, but the market was not ready for them. Next time, I wanted to know whether the customers actually existed before building the whole solution.

That became the beginning of InFact — and of a method I still believe in: Ask first. Understand the need. Then build.

The common thread

Ideas seek people

Perhaps you are not meant to build the idea alone.

Some ideas do not lack potential. They lack the right combination of people: a co-founder, an investor, a specialist partner, a pilot customer or someone who can take responsibility for bringing the idea to life.

Every idea should be clear about what it needs.
  • What problem does the idea solve?
  • What has already been researched or built?
  • What kind of person or organisation is missing?
  • What is the realistic next step?
Seeking

Co-founder

A person who will build the company and share responsibility, risk and ownership.

Seeking

Investor

Capital and experience for an idea that has already demonstrated a need or early traction.

Seeking

Specialist partner

Technical, legal, commercial or industry-specific expertise the idea is missing.

Seeking

Pilot customer

A real user willing to test the solution and help make it useful.
