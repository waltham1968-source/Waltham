Facility management · customers · practical laboratory

LBC Facility Management

LBC FM is Waltham’s ongoing business foundation and practical laboratory. It brings together commercial letting, facility management, tenant relations, local partnerships and support for smaller companies on one platform with many different tenants and real needs.

This gives us a valuable environment in which new concepts, AI tools and ways of working can be tested on a smaller scale, adjusted and either adopted or set aside. The experience continually updates our own knowledge of what works in practice.

When we use AI, the work therefore does not begin with an empty question. It is grounded in context from real processes, properties, customers and decisions. The technology does not become magically smarter — but the questions become sharper, and the answers more relevant and useful.
