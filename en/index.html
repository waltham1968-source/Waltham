Our method

WHAT · WHO · PROOF

A simple communication method: connect what the business offers with what the customer needs, supported by evidence that can be checked.

WHAT

What do you offer?

Describe the task, product or expertise so customers can understand what you can help them with.

WHO

Who is it for?

Start with a specific customer, a recognisable problem and the situation in which your help makes a difference.

PROOF

What supports the claim?

Show an actual experience, completed task or original study. Distinguish customer experience, company statements and measured results.

Trust before insight.

We start with useful examples and publicly available information. A deeper review of internal matters happens only when you want it, with an agreed purpose, access and scope.

Discretion is part of the collaboration. Client information and results are made public only with explicit permission.

A story also needs to reach its readers.

We bring together research, fact-checking, an article, an illustration and a short sharing text. The story gets a permanent address on an owned website and can be shared by the business, customers and others who find it relevant.

Owned publishing and voluntary sharing are our starting point. Independent editorial coverage is different and cannot be promised. We follow relevant enquiries and documented outcomes, not just clicks.
