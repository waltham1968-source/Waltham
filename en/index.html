How we work

Embedded with the client.

The assignment, scope and price are agreed from the outset. We work in manageable stages and involve specialists when the task requires it.

Your own systems

Wherever possible, we work from your own accounts, tools and workflows. Access, data handling and any external services are clarified as part of the assignment.

Control and ownership

Your material, accounts and configuration should remain under your control. We clarify rights and supplier terms so it is clear what you own and what the solution depends on.

Handover and training

Documentation, training and support are agreed as needed. The aim is for you to manage everyday use yourselves. Maintenance needs and continued assistance are described openly.

Discretion is part of the collaboration. Confidential material is shared only by agreement and only to the extent required by the assignment. Client names, assignments and results are used publicly only with explicit permission.

The experience behind Waltham

From idea to company.

Waltham’s professional and entrepreneurial roots go back to 1994.

That year, Nikolai Waltham graduated from Norges Markedshøyskole with a diploma thesis in public relations and communication. The thesis was not about a hypothetical company, but about the business he intended to establish himself.

The thesis was therefore adapted into an application for start-up funding from Statens nærings- og distriktsutviklingsfond (SND), one of the predecessors of today’s Innovation Norway. The application was approved, and the funding made it possible to establish Ski- og Sykkelstasjonen.

The company was conceived as more than a traditional retail business. The ambition was to develop and manufacture skis and bicycles, and in its early years the company produced, among other things, its own skis.

This marked the beginning of a way of working that still characterises Waltham: taking an idea, examining whether it is viable, articulating it clearly and turning it into a working business.

SmartSense followed, working with gas detection and automation, and in 2001 came the research company InFact. Nikolai Waltham was behind its concept, method and technology, as well as its name, positioning and marketing. InFact was built around proprietary technology for automated telephone surveys and grew through the usefulness of its solution, client relationships and recommendations.

Following its sale in 2024, InFact continues under new ownership. Today, more than 30 years of experience in developing ideas, technology, market research and building companies are applied to advisory work and practical development for other businesses.
