A different kind of visibility

Being found is not enough. You also need to be understood and chosen.

When customers ask an AI assistant for recommendations, the answer is not necessarily a list of links. It increasingly involves interpreting needs and suggesting relevant options.

Effective AI visibility does not start with a technical trick. It starts with a company that can explain what it does, whom it helps and why it deserves trust. Websites, content and technology are tools to express that identity – not goals in themselves.

01 · Self-image

What you say.

Who are you? What do you promise? Is your value clear?

02 · Reputation

What others say.

How do customers, partners and independent sources describe you?

03 · Reality

What you deliver.

Can you support your claims with evidence? Trust depends on consistency.

Self-image · Reputation · Reality is Waltham's strategic framework, not a claim about an AI system's exact ranking algorithm.

Waltham's professional roots reach back to a Norwegian technology and research venture established in 2001. Working across technology, communication and market research, the original company became Waltham & Co AS in 2003.

The work has since encompassed company formation, market research, software, communication and business development in Norway and Denmark. Today that experience informs a particular focus on AI Visibility.

Broad experience without pretending to do everything ourselves.

Waltham combines strategic sparring with a network of specialists in research, content, design, websites and technology. When a project calls for more expertise, we help shape the right collaboration.
