2000–2025 · Systems · market insight · scaling

InFact

InFact began with a concrete question: How can large-scale data collection be made faster, more accessible and manageable from anywhere?

The answer was an automated telephone system and a company combining technology, analysis and communication. The polls created insight for clients, news for the media and self-reinforcing momentum for the company. Today, InFact continues as a Nordic analysis and communications agency.

Challenge
Make manual data collection scalable
Resultat
A system and a company built for more than two decades of market insight
Principle
Ask first. Understand the need. Then build.

Complex case · digital case room

Bevar Nyborg Fjord

An extensive local planning case was spread across documents, consultation responses, authority decisions, news articles and different versions of the story. This made the case difficult to understand — even for people who wanted to get involved.

We gathered the material in a digital case room with a clear timeline, themes, sources and explanations. The result is an open knowledge base that makes complex information searchable, verifiable and easier to act on.
