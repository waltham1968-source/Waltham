What Waltham works on

Waltham is a small, independent advisory firm. We step into situations where the direction is not obvious: an idea that needs testing, a business with untapped potential or a technology that must be translated into practical value.

AI is an important tool in the work, but never the starting point. We begin with the business, the people and the question that actually needs answering.
