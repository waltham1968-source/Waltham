# Waltham communication positioning — 31 October 2026

Waltham is a research-driven communications agency with AI visibility as a central purpose. The public offer prioritises research, documented storytelling and AI visibility. Existing strategy/advisory and visibility products remain accessible.

The method is WHAT–WHO–PROOF (HVAD–HVEM–BEVIS in Danish). It is Waltham's communication framework, not a search engine ranking model. Trust comes before access to internal client information. Research and human fact-checking precede publication. Owned publishing and voluntary sharing are distinct from independent editorial coverage.
